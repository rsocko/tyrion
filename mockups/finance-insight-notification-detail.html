Policy-suppressed

Tyrion policy

A separately confirmed Tyrion policy suppresses matching future occurrences for a bounded scope and duration.

Show scope, expiration, actor, policy version, reason code, and undo route.

Stale or unavailable

Source warning

Monarch coverage is stale, partial, or unavailable. Last reliable analysis: Aug 9, 6:00 PM.

Do not create fresh alerts from incomplete data. Keep prior insight visible with a warning.

Two state machines: Tyrion source lifecycle is analyzing → open → resolved/superseded. Mission Control delivery disposition is unread/read, snoozed, or dismissed. Policy suppression is a separate confirmed Tyrion action. Insufficient and unavailable are explicit non-alert states.
