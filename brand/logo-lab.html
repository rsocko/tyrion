02 · Optical cuts

Three cuts, chosen by size

Fine detail that reads beautifully at 96px turns to noise at 16px. So the mark ships in three cuts. The renderer picks by target size — the silhouette stays constant, the interior detail scales down.

Full

≥ 56px. Reeded edge, inner ring, radial emboss, true high-contrast serif T. The signature.

Simplified

24–52px. Reeding drops, but the inner ring stays for coin depth; T grown ~20% and re-centered.

Micro

≤ 20px. Inner ring gone, but a solid coin rim stays so it never reads as a bare disc; T fattened with a stroke so serifs survive.

Wrong  Full cut forced down to small sizes — reeding shimmers, T thins out and vanishes.
Right  Micro cut at the same sizes — a solid, legible coin with a strong T.

08 · Rules

Clear space & don’ts

Clear space = half the coin’s diameter on every side. Minimum size = 16px (micro cut).
  • Don’t recolor the T to a status hue (red/green/amber).
  • Don’t stretch, skew, or rotate the coin.
  • Don’t add a Lannister lion or heraldry — the reference stays light.
  • Don’t place gold coin on amber warning fill (they blur together).
  • Don’t use the Full cut below 56px — switch to Simplified / Micro.
  • Don’t swap Cormorant for another serif in the T.
