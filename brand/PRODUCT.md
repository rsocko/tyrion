# PRODUCT.md — Tyrion

> Durable product truth. Captured via Impeccable `init`. Visual decisions live in DESIGN.md.

## What it is
Tyrion is a personal/household finance agent **and** UI. Like OWL, it is a single
character-name that is simultaneously the product you **open**, the agent you
**ask**, and the autonomous voice that **acts**. Named for the sharpest, most
honest Master of Coin in fiction.

## Who it's for
One household (the maker's family). Personal use; open-source at most — never a
shipped commercial product. This removes trademark/collision pressure and lets
the Game-of-Thrones reference stand as light seasoning, not a liability.

## Jobs to be done
- **Open** — dashboard, transaction triage, per-kid attribution, bill reconciliation, forecasts.
- **Ask** — natural-language finance chat ("Can we afford the trip?", "Any forgotten subscriptions?").
- **Acts** — autonomous alerts: duplicate charges, bills due, budget breaches, clean-week summaries.

## Systems it connects to
- **Monarch Money** — source of transactions/accounts (sync).
- **Mission Control** — the hub/"small council"; Tyrion emits `TYRION`-labeled alerts into its feed.
- **OWL (Document Intelligence)** — the "Maester"; docs/knowledge counterpart. Tyrion handles money, OWL handles documents.
- Surfaces its own insights (forecasting, subscription audits, kid budgets).

## Modes (Impeccable)
- App shell / dashboard / triage / settings → **Operate** (scanability, consistency, real usage scene win).
- Any future landing/readme page → **Persuade**.

## Non-negotiables
- **Family-safe.** Kids appear in the app. GoT wit stays PG; no off-color lines.
- **Status colors are sacred.** Red/amber/green/blue mean status only.
- **Gold is brand, not status.** ~5% usage; never adjacent to amber warning.
- Fits the Mission Control family look: dark-first, restrained, one accent.
