# CFinance Brand — Round 4: Single Name (agent = product)

**Direction:** back to a **single name** (the Round-2 field), not a product+agent pair. The thing *is* an agent as much as a UI — so, like **OWL**, one name has to carry three jobs at once:

1. **Open** it — works as app title / nav / favicon (the product).
2. **Ask** it — works as a name you talk to (the agent).
3. It **acts** — works as a sender/voice: “[Name] found…”, “[Name] set aside…”

That test kills pure *place/system* words (Pantry, Harbor, Oikos, Cadence, Almanac — you don’t “ask Pantry” comfortably) and rewards names that are **characters or verbs**. Personal use → collisions de-weighted; optimize for delight + fits the OWL universe.

---

## Group A — Character names (a person you talk to; strongest agent feel)

### ★ TYRION
- **Open:** “Tyrion” in the nav. **Ask:** “Ask Tyrion if we can afford the trip.” **Acts:** “Tyrion flagged a duplicate charge.”
- **Why single-name works:** he’s a *character* (so the agent voice is native) who is *literally the Master of Coin* (so the finance meaning is native). Same shape as OWL: one character-name = the whole product. Your favorite thread.
- **Tagline:** *“Always pays your debts.”* **Voice:** dry, candid, clever. **Palette:** gold on charcoal + oxblood. **Avatar:** coin-monogram “T” / small sharp silhouette.
- **OWL fit:** two clever characters in one realm (wise owl + witty Master of Coin). **Note:** HBO IP (fine personal); Tyrion himself = honest underdog, no greed baggage.

### TYLAND
- Deep-cut Master of Coin (House of the Dragon). Same logic as Tyrion but **feels more “yours”** and reads almost like a coined name. “Ask Tyland.” “Tyland reconciled the month.” Less instantly recognizable = more private.

### BALIN
- The kindly dwarf keeper of the hoard — warmer, less famous, easy to say. “Balin found a leak in the budget.” Feeds the dwarf-gold thread without Thorin’s greed.

## Group B — Creature names (a single animal-character, the closest sibling to OWL)

### ★ SQUIRREL
- **Open:** “Squirrel.” **Ask:** “Ask Squirrel what we can stash this month.” **Acts:** “Squirrel tucked $200 toward the quarterly bill.”
- **Why:** the universal *saver* — stores up for winter = exactly what a reserves/savings agent does. And a creature-name **mirrors OWL directly** (Athena’s owl ↔ your thrifty squirrel) — the two apps become one animal-kingdom. Meaning-perfect and delightful.
- **Voice:** industrious, cheerful, forward-looking. **Palette:** chestnut + nut-brown + cream. **Avatar:** minimal squirrel (acorn = a saved dollar). **Risk:** reads *cute* (great personally, less “serious”).

### FOX
- Clever, resourceful “good with what it’s got.” Short, sharp, pairs with OWL as another animal. “Fox sniffed out a forgotten subscription.” Slightly trickster-coded.

### MAGPIE
- Collects coins/shiny things — vivid, but carries a faint *hoarding/steals* connotation. Included for range.

## Group C — Persona/first-names (warm human agent; no fiction baggage)

### ★ REMY
- **Open/Ask/Acts:** “Open Remy.” · “Ask Remy where the grocery money went.” · “Remy found a duplicate subscription.” Likeable steward, ages well, not fiction-bound. **Palette:** warm ink + teal/plum. **Avatar:** “r” roundel.

### BAILEY
- Bailiff/estate-steward + protected courtyard — care + guarding in one warm name. “Bailey caught a double-payment.” Trustworthy, gender-neutral.

### WILLOW
- Resilient, gentle, tending. “Willow set aside a little each week.” Warm + calming; also faintly nature (OWL-adjacent).

### MABEL
- Grandmother-keeps-the-books warmth; highly memorable. Risk: very homey.

## Group D — Verb-agents (a name that *does* something; acts natively, less “talk-to”)

### ★ GLEAN
- **Acts:** “Glean found $40/mo in forgotten subscriptions.” **Ask:** “Ask Glean what changed.” Two meanings both fit: gather stray amounts **+** extract insight. Superb agent-verb; slightly abstract as a “who.”

### PLUMB
- “Plumb says the books are level and true.” Reconciliation-as-truth; precise, grounded.

### TALLY
- “Tally squared up the month.” The running record personified; friendly, plain.

### KEEL
- “Keel kept you on an even keel.” Stability agent; calm, short, clean.

## Group E — Invented (ownable, product-gives-it-meaning)

### SELA
- “Ask Sela.” · “Sela set aside $200.” Calm, brandable; carries a quiet “steady + reflect” resonance. Neutral enough to be persona *or* product.
